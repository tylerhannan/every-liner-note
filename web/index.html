Reading every liner note at once

I read liner notes. Who played on it, where it was pressed, what colour the vinyl is, how long it runs. Discogs has the liner notes for releases, and I have records. So I loaded all of Discogs into ClickHouse to see what my shelf looks like next to everyone else's.

What's it pressed on?

Limited vinyl tells you its colour in words, and Discogs has different ways of saying it: "coke bottle green", "arctic pearl", "pink [baby pink]". Nobody's writing a regex for that.
